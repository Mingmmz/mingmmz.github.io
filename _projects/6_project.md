## Project Overview

**Animal Rhapsody** is a creative animation developed for a **Uniqlo UT campaign concept** as part of the **2022 National College Student Advertising Art Competition**.

The project explores generative visual design through **Processing** and **p5.js**, combining animated graphics, rhythm, and visual storytelling to create a playful promotional experience.
