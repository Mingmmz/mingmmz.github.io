Find Your Sister!

A maze FPS game published on Roblox Studio, coorperated with Tencent Game Institute (2021)

Project Overview

Find Your Sister! is a maze-based FPS adventure game developed and published using Roblox Studio in collaboration with the Tencent Game Institute.

The game combines exploration, NPC interaction, item trading, combat, and environmental storytelling. Players navigate through a maze, interact with characters in a specific sequence, acquire the items and weapons needed to defeat monsters, and ultimately rescue the protagonist’s sister.

Game Story

A young boy becomes separated from his sister during an outing and must navigate a mysterious maze to find her.

The player’s objective is simple:

Find your sister and escape the maze.

Along the way, the player encounters monsters and several NPCs. Each NPC provides information, items, or access to new parts of the storyline. Players must interact with these characters in the correct sequence, collect necessary objects, trade for weapons, and eventually defeat the final boss to rescue the protagonist’s sister.

Maze and Gameplay

Overview of the maze structure and locations of the major NPCs and objectives.

The maze map uses different colors to indicate important characters and objectives:

  • Blue: Guides
  • Orange: Know-it-All
  • Pink: Sprite
  • Green: Pudding
  • Triangle: Location of the corresponding quest object

The gameplay follows several core rules:

  • NPCs must be encountered in a specific order to progress through the storyline.
  • Players collect and exchange items with NPCs to obtain weapons and other necessary objects.
  • Monsters block portions of the maze and must be defeated to continue.
  • The maze ultimately leads toward a single path to the final encounter.

Scene Design

The game features two primary environmental styles designed around the personalities and roles of the NPCs: Forest and Candy.

The Forest and Candy environments provide contrasting visual styles for different regions of the game world.

NPC Design

Guide

The Guide introduces the player to the maze and directs them toward Know-it-All, initiating the main quest sequence.

Know-it-All

Know-it-All exchanges treasures found by the player for weapons required to defeat monsters encountered later in the maze.

Sprite

Sprite guides the player toward Pudding and provides access to magic water needed during the final boss encounter.

Pudding

Pudding directs the player toward hidden treasures and exchanges collected items for objects necessary to continue the quest.

Boss

The final boss is responsible for hiding the protagonist's sister. Players must use the items and abilities acquired throughout the maze to defeat the boss and complete the game.
